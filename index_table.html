ABOUT INDIA

India is a diverse country located in South Asia. Its capital is New Delhi. India is known for its rich culture, ancient history, beautiful landscapes, festivals, and delicious food. It is the world's largest democracy and is home to people who speak many different languages. India is also famous for its historical monuments, such as the Taj Mahal, and its national symbols, including the tiger and the peacock.
